# SCI Introduction Writing Skill

## Purpose
Help an AI write an SCI Introduction that **moves from broad field context to a specific research gap, then ends with a clear objective, hypothesis, and prediction**.

## When to Use
Use this skill when drafting or revising the Introduction section of an original research paper.

## Inputs Required
Provide:
- research field
- specific topic
- key concepts or terms
- relevant literature findings
- current knowledge gap
- study objective
- hypotheses / predictions

## Output Goal
Produce an Introduction that explains **what the problem is, why it matters, what is known, what is unknown, and what this study will do**.

## Core Structure
The AI should follow a narrowing funnel:
1. broad significance of the field
2. specific research topic
3. key terms / conceptual framing
4. relevant prior studies
5. knowledge gap
6. study objective, hypothesis, prediction

## Writing Rules
1. Open with the importance of the field or problem.
2. Move gradually from broad context to the exact research question.
3. Define necessary technical or debated terms.
4. Use only literature directly relevant to the study.
5. Focus the literature review on identifying a gap.
6. End the section with explicit objectives, hypotheses, and predictions.
7. Keep logical flow consistent with the rest of the paper.

## AI Workflow
### Step 1: Set the context
Explain the larger field significance in 1-2 concise paragraphs.

### Step 2: Position the topic
Narrow to the specific phenomenon, mechanism, population, or problem.

### Step 3: Define terms
Clarify technical concepts or contested terminology needed for the paper.

### Step 4: Review only relevant literature
Summarize the most pertinent studies and show what remains unresolved.

### Step 5: State the gap
Make the missing knowledge concrete and researchable.

### Step 6: Land on the study aim
End with:
- objective(s)
- hypothesis / hypotheses
- prediction(s)
- optional numbered objectives if the paper uses them consistently

## Recommended Output Format
### Paragraph 1
Field significance and broad problem

### Paragraph 2
Specific topic and conceptual framing

### Paragraph 3
Relevant prior work and unresolved issue

### Final Paragraph
Research gap, study objective, hypotheses, predictions

## Quality Checklist
The AI must check:
- Does the Introduction narrow logically?
- Are the key terms defined?
- Is the literature review selective and purposeful?
- Is the knowledge gap explicit?
- Are objectives and predictions clearly stated?
- Does the Introduction avoid turning into a full literature review?

## Do Not
- write a general textbook overview
- include a bloated literature dump
- mention studies without explaining relevance
- leave the gap implicit
- end without a concrete objective

```text

Task:
Write a focused Introduction that goes from background to research gap to study objective.

Study information:
- Field:

- logical funnel structure
- selective literature use
- clear gap statement

```
