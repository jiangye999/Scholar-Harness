# SCI Conclusion Writing Skill

## Purpose
Help an AI write a concise SCI Conclusion that **summarizes the study as a whole, states its importance, and avoids repeating detailed results**.

## When to Use
Use this skill when the target journal requires:
- a stand-alone Conclusion section
- highlights / takeaway bullets
- a closing paragraph at the end of the Discussion

## Inputs Required
Provide:
- study objective
- overall finding
- main contribution
- practical or theoretical importance
- optional future direction
- journal preference for format

## Output Goal
Produce a brief, declarative ending that tells the reader what the study contributes and why it matters.

## Writing Rules
1. Use declarative, summative language.
2. Keep the Conclusion short.
3. Do not repeat specific result details.
4. Do not restate the entire Discussion.
5. Focus on the study as a whole.
6. Emphasize importance to the field.
7. Use 3-5 sentences unless the journal requests another format.

## AI Workflow
### Step 1: Restate the study purpose in one line
What was this study about?

### Step 2: Summarize the main answer
What did the study show overall?

### Step 3: State the contribution
Why does this matter scientifically or practically?

### Step 4: Add a future-looking line if useful
Only if it adds value and fits the journal style.

## Recommended Output Format
### Standard Conclusion
- Sentence 1: Study aim
- Sentence 2: Overall finding
- Sentence 3: Contribution / implication
- Sentence 4: Optional future direction

## Quality Checklist
The AI must check:
- Is the Conclusion concise?
- Does it summarize the study at a high level?
- Does it avoid detailed numerical repetition?
- Does it clearly state importance?
- Does it fit the journal format?

## Do Not
- paste the Results again
- introduce new evidence
- broaden the claim beyond the manuscript
- end weakly or vaguely
